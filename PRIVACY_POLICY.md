## 1. Who this covers

THE ROT is a local-party social deduction game. Players join a shared game
room using a short room code and play together in person, using their own
phones. There is no user account system.

## 2. What information we collect

- **Display name.** When you join a room, you choose a display name. This is
  shown to other players in that room only, for the duration of that game.
- **In-game actions.** Your movements, votes, chat messages sent during the
  discussion phase, and game choices are transmitted to our server so the
  game can function, and relayed live to other players in your room.
- **Technical data.** Our server temporarily processes your device's IP
  address as part of normal network operation (this is standard for any
  real-time multiplayer connection) and to apply basic anti-abuse rate
  limiting (e.g. preventing one device from creating an excessive number of
  rooms in a short time). We do not log or store IP addresses beyond what's
  needed for that immediate technical purpose.

## 3. What we do NOT collect

- No account registration, email address, or phone number.
- No real name (only the display name you choose to type in).
- No location data beyond the coarse, standard information any internet
  connection reveals (e.g. country-level, from your IP) — the game itself
  has no location features.
- No advertising identifiers, and we do not show ads.
- No payment information — the game is free with no in-app purchases.

## 4. How long data is kept

- Display names, positions, votes, and chat messages exist only in server
  memory for the lifetime of the game room. They are not written to a
  database.
- When a game room ends, or after a period of inactivity, its data is
  deleted from server memory. Nothing about a finished game persists
  afterward.
- Rate-limiting counters (used to prevent abuse) are kept for a short
  rolling window (currently up to 10 minutes) and then discarded
  automatically.

## 5. Who we share data with

We do not sell or share player data with third parties. Data sent to our
server is only relayed to other players within the same game room, which is
necessary for the game itself to function.

THE ROT does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The
game requires no account and only asks for a display name, which players
should be advised not to make identifying (e.g., not their real full name).

## 7. Your choices

Because there's no persistent account, there's no data to request deletion
of after a game ends — it's already gone. During an active game, leaving the
room removes your presence from other players' views going forward.
