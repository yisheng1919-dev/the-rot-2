## Full description (4000 characters max)

```
THE ROT is a fast, tense party game of hidden identity for 4 to 12 players
— played together in the same room, each on your own phone.

One of you is secretly THE ORIGINAL CORRUPTED. Everyone else starts as an
Innocent detective aboard a damaged ship. Nobody knows who's who — including
you, if you get robbed.

HOW IT PLAYS
- Explore the ship. There are no tasks to grind through — just walk around,
  talk to each other out loud, and watch who's acting strange.
- Every round, the power goes out. Get a group of players to the Power Room
  and restore it together, or the ship stays dark.
- The Corrupted can sneak up on nearby players and steal their cards. Get
  robbed and survive, and you'll face a private, one-time choice: stay
  Innocent, or secretly join the Corrupted and gain the steal ability
  yourself starting next round.
- Run out of cards, and you're eliminated — but you can keep watching as a
  ghost.
- After power's restored, gather to discuss — in person, or with the
  built-in chat — and vote out who you suspect.

WHO WINS
- Innocents win if the Original Corrupted gets voted out.
- The Corrupted win if their leader survives all 3 rounds.

WHAT YOU NEED
- 4 to 12 players, each with their own phone
- Everyone in the same physical room (or on a call) to talk during
  discussions
- No sign-up, no accounts — just pick a display name and join with a room
  code

THE ROT is completely free to play, with no ads and no in-app purchases.
```
